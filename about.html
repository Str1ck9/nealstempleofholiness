Core Beliefs & Values

  • Biblical Foundation & Salvation - We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the foundation for faith and daily living. Salvation is found through Jesus Christ, who offers forgiveness, new life, and reconciliation with God to all who believe.
  • Holiness & Spirit-Led Living - We believe God calls His people to live holy lives, set apart for His purpose. Through the power and guidance of the Holy Spirit, we seek to walk in obedience, love, and continuous transformation into Christ's likeness.
  • Community, Service & Compassion - We believe the church is called to serve others with love, humility, and compassion. Through outreach, service, and justice, we seek to strengthen our community and demonstrate God's love in practical ways.
  • Worship, Prayer & Discipleship - We value heartfelt worship, faithful prayer, and intentional discipleship. As we grow together in faith, we strive to deepen our relationship with God, equip one another, and reflect Christ in every area of life.
